Research

Computational systems biology for biomedical discovery

We design computational models and data-integration frameworks to understand disease mechanisms and identify therapeutic opportunities.

Drug repositioning

Systematic discovery of new indications for existing drugs using biological networks, omics signatures, and deep learning.

  • Drug–disease association prediction
  • Network embedding
  • Heterogeneous biomedical data integration

Single-cell genomics

Computational pipelines for interpreting cell heterogeneity and regulatory programs from single-cell datasets.

  • Cell clustering and annotation
  • Differential expression
  • Regulatory network inference

Metagenomics

Microbiome data analysis for taxonomic profiling, feature discovery, and disease prediction.

  • Taxonomic classification
  • Microbiome-based prediction
  • Computational pipeline development
