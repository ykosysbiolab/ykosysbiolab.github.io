Research

Research Topics

Computational systems biology methods for drug repositioning, single-cell genomics, metagenomics, and LLM-based personalized disease-risk prediction.

Personalized disease-risk prediction

We model an individual's genomic variants with GPT/BERT-style language models and nucleotide-transformer-style DNA foundation models to estimate variant pathogenicity, integrate biomedical evidence, and predict disease-specific risk profiles.

GPT/BERTNucleotide TransformerPathogenicityDisease Risk

Systematic drug repositioning

We combine chemical structures of known disease drugs, CT/ET target proteins, protein–protein interaction networks, and biomedical LLM embeddings to prioritize new drug–disease associations.

Chemical StructurePPILLM EmbeddingRepurposing

Single-cell genomics

We analyze single-cell RNA-seq data to identify disease- or growth-associated shifts in cell-type composition, cellular states, and regulatory programs.

scRNA-seqCell DistributionDisease StateGrowth

Metagenomics

We compare metagenomic profiles between disease cohorts and healthy controls to discover taxonomic signatures and build microbiome-based disease prediction models.
